# Privacy Policy: AoE4 Esports Tournament Ranking (ATR) app

Last updated: 25 September 2026

**The app does not collect, store or share personal information about Reddit users.** It does not read your username, track what you view or record your activity.

## What the app stores

In the app's storage on Reddit's platform (per subreddit installation):

- The ranking table and tournament match history copied from the public ATR Google Sheet (player names as published by the ATR, ratings, results, countries).
- A cache, kept for up to one hour, of public ranked-ladder data fetched from AoE4World.
- Links between ATR player names and AoE4World profiles that subreddit moderators choose to set.

## External requests

The app's server makes read-only requests to:

- `docs.google.com`, to read the public ATR sheet.
- `aoe4world.com`, to read public ladder data for the player being viewed.

No Reddit user data is included in these requests.

## Players listed in the ranking

Player names, countries and results come from public tournament records compiled by the ATR team. A player who wants their entry corrected or removed should contact the ATR team; the app reflects the sheet at its next sync.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the app developer through Reddit messages or the ATR Discord.
